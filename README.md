This website is a personal fan page dedicated to Black Clover, an anime that I have really enjoyed watching. The main idea behind creating this website is to have a place where I can express my thoughts and opinions about the series. I want to talk about the characters, their personalities, the storyline, different magic abilities, and some of my favourite moments from the anime. Asta's determination, Yuno's talent, the Black Bulls, and the journey towards becoming the Wizard King are some of the things that make Black Clover so interesting to me. I also want to share my favourite characters, personal rankings, theories, and views on different events throughout the story.

The original inspiration for the website's design came from Boba Bash Delhi. I really liked the dark theme used at the event, and it gave me an idea for the overall look of my website. I wanted to use a similar dark aesthetic because I felt it would suit the kind of atmosphere I had in mind. The theme became one of the first things I thought about while planning the website. I started imagining how the background, colours, fonts, and different sections would look together.

Around that time, I was also watching Black Clover, and I thought it would be a great idea to make the website about it. I was enjoying the anime and had a lot of thoughts about its characters and storyline that I wanted to share. Gradually, the idea of making a simple fan page turned into a proper website project. I wanted to include elements from the anime and give the entire website a look that matched its magical world and my own design preferences.

Eventually, I decided to bring the idea to life and start building the website. While working on it, I got the opportunity to experiment with HTML, CSS, layouts, videos (just some higlights waht made me like black clover), and other web design elements. I also wanted the website to feel personal, so the content would reflect my actual opinions and experiences as someone who watches the anime. Overall, this project is something I created out of my interest in Black Clover and web development. It is a small way of combining two things I enjoy: watching anime and creating websites.
